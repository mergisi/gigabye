# Gigabye: Privacy Policy & Support

_Last updated: October 15, 2026_

**Support:** questions, bugs or refund help: email **mergisi@gmail.com**. Refunds for App Store purchases are handled by Apple at https://reportaproblem.apple.com.

Gigabye ("the app") is a photo-cleanup app. **Your photos never leave your iPhone.**

## Your photos
- The app reads your photo library only after you allow it, and only to find screenshots, duplicates and similar photos.
- All analysis, including sizes, thumbnails, image fingerprints and similarity, happens on your device. Photos, thumbnails and fingerprints are never uploaded.
- To make rescans faster, the app saves a small cache of image fingerprints in its own storage on your device. Deleting the app deletes the cache.
- Photos are deleted only when you confirm in the iOS system prompt. Deleted items go to Recently Deleted and can be recovered for 30 days.

## Anonymous usage statistics
To learn which features help, the app sends anonymous usage events to our analytics provider, Mixpanel. This data is **not linked to your identity** and is **not used for tracking** across apps or websites. You can turn it off at any time in **Settings › Share anonymous usage stats**. When you turn it off, events that haven't been sent yet are deleted and nothing more is sent.

**What is collected**
- A random ID created when you first open the app. It's stored only in this app and isn't connected to your name, Apple ID or device advertising ID, and it's reset if you delete the app.
- Events: app installed and opened, scan started and completed, items deleted, free limit reached, paywall shown, trial started, purchase, restore, share card shown and shared.
- Event details: counts and sizes (for example, the number of screenshots, bytes freed and scan duration), a rough library-size range (such as "1k–5k"), where a deletion came from (screenshots, duplicates, similar or swipe), the product purchased and its price, app version and iOS version, and when the event happened.

**What is never collected**
- Your photos, thumbnails, image fingerprints, file names, locations or photo metadata.
- Your name, email, contacts or Apple ID.
- The advertising identifier (IDFA). The app does not ask for tracking permission and does not track you.
- Your IP address. We tell Mixpanel not to record or geolocate it (`ip=0`). Like any internet request, the connection itself reveals an IP address to Mixpanel's servers.

## Purchases
Purchases are handled by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. Purchase events in the anonymous statistics include only the product and price.

## Sharing
If you choose to share a cleanup card, the iOS share sheet sends an image containing only numbers (space freed, counts, dates) to the app you pick. The card never contains your photos.

## Children
The app does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, including children. Usage statistics are anonymous.

## Changes
If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at this address and in the app's App Store listing.
